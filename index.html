        This app only records the numbers your scale shows. It doesn't judge them or give health advice, and it isn't a medical device.

        Your weigh-ins are saved on this phone only, and the app never uploads them. Camera pictures are only looked at while you read a number; they're never saved or sent. No account or cookies. The app adds no analytics scripts and doesn't track you. Our host, Vercel, keeps request logs that can include IP addresses and the site you came from. We only look at weekly totals of requests to load the app, grouped by that site, to see whether people find it. We don't look at individual requests.
